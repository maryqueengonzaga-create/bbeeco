BbeeCo Task Management — How to Use

1. Task Categories (Following JIS Discipline)

Daily Tasks — recurring routines, mga ginagawa araw-araw (cleaning, reports, opening/closing).
Special Tasks — one-off or project-based assignments (campaigns, audits, client visits).
Scheduled Tasks — time-bound deliverables (deadline meetings, monthly reports).

1b. Roles vs Tasks (IMPORTANT — JIS Standardized Work)

Para sa mga trabaho na rotating (live selling, cooking, cashier), gamitin ang Roles at Shift Roster — hindi Tasks. Ang Role ay permanent (e.g., "Breakfast Cook"), ang Shift Slot ay assigns role sa time + person (e.g., "6-9 AM Breakfast Cook → Roberto"). Bukas, ibang tao sa parehong slot. Pareho ang SOP, kaya pareho ang quality output. Yan ang Hyōjun Sagyō (JIS Z 8141).

1c. Handover Log — Lahat ng Task Transfer Dapat Naka-log

Pag may biglang lipat ng task mula Employee A patungong Employee B, dapat may Handover Log entry: ano natapos, ano pending, anong ire-remind. Rule of thumb: "Walang task transfer without 2-line handover note." Otherwise, lost ang accountability.

2. Priority Matrix — Alisin ang Less Priority

Lagi mong ilagay ang task sa tamang quadrant. Goal: palitan ng oras sa Q4 patungong Q2. Q4 tasks dapat i-eliminate o automate.

3. KPI Scoring (JIS Q 9001)

Hindi lang quantity — pati quality. Every completed task may rating (1-5). Composite Score = Quantity 30% + Quality 35% + On-Time 20% + 5S 10% + Kaizen 5%.

4. 5S Audit

Weekly audit ng workplace condition: Seiri (Sort), Seiton (Set in order), Seiso (Shine), Seiketsu (Standardize), Shitsuke (Sustain). Score 1-10 per area.

5. PDCA + Kaizen

Para sa improvement projects, gumamit ng PDCA cycle. Para sa daily small improvements, mag-submit ng Kaizen suggestion. Lahat ng employee dapat mag-contribute.

6. Data Storage

Lahat ng data ay naka-save sa browser mo (localStorage). Walang internet needed. Pwedeng i-export sa CSV anytime. Para sa team-wide use, i-host sa shared server.

8. Hours & Payroll Rules

Per-Day Hours Computation:

  • Regular Hours (regHrs) = sum ng dtr_logs.total_hours sa araw (time-in to time-out)
  • Live Hours (liveHrs) = sum ng live_sessions.duration_hours sa araw
  • Total = regHrs + liveHrs
  • 8-hour daily cap: kung total > 8, regHrs trim with live priority (live always counted full)
  • Break deduction: Kung total ≥ 8 hours → -1 hour from regHrs. Kung ≥ 6 hours → -0.5 hour.
  • Late deduction (from schedules.{day}_start):
    • 0-5 mins late: grace period, walang deduction
    • 6-15 mins late: 15-min deduction
    • 16-30 mins late: 30-min deduction
    • >30 mins late: actual minutes deducted
  • Overtime (OT): Pwede mag-time-in with type='ot' for OT hours. Currently treated as regular hours sa payroll engine — OT-specific rate at request flow ay future feature.

Per-Cutoff Pay Computation:

  • regPay = max(0, totalRegHrs - lateHrs) × reg_rate (per-employee, default ₱62.50/hr)
  • livePay = totalLiveHrs × live_rate (per-employee, default ₱83.33/hr)
  • Allowance = (weekly_allowance × distinct weeks worked) + cutoff_allowance
  • Gross Pay = regPay + livePay + allowance
  • Deductions: SSS (₱581.30 default OR from deduction_group), PhilHealth (2.5% or group %), Pag-IBIG (₱100 or group), late × reg_rate, break × reg_rate, cash_advance per_cutoff, item_charges
  • Net Pay = Gross - Deductions

💡 Cutoffs: Days 1-15 and 16-end of month. Compute happens on day 15 and last day. Dashboard shows red alert "Run Cutoff Now" button when due.

9. Quality Principles Map

Bawat module sa system na ito ay naka-align sa specific quality management principles:

Standardized Work — consistent procedures → Roles & SOPs, Schedules
Production Leveling — balanced workload → Shift Roster
Handover Protocol — structured task transfers → Handover Log
Visual Management — at-a-glance status → BbeeCo Dashboard, Live DTR, kiosk status badges
5S — Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, Sustain → 5S Audit
PDCA — Plan-Do-Check-Act → PDCA Tracker
Continuous Improvement — small daily wins → Improvement Log + Weekly Check-In auto-convert
Self-Reflection — review work weekly → Weekly Check-Ins (challenge + concerns + wins)
Quality Management — quality + quantity scoring → Composite KPI scoring, payroll quality compensation
Error Proofing — prevent mistakes → 2-second hold confirmations, time-in-required-before-live, end-live-before-time-out
Stop the Line — escalate problems → Account locking, incident flagging, missing check-in alerts
